# Samyoga Jyotish · Book I

**Historical beta edition · v1.0β · UA · 2026**  
**Status:** `CLOSED PROJECT · ARCHIVAL / OVERVIEW USE`

`Samyoga Jyotish` is a completed and closed research project. No further work is being conducted under the Samyoga name. Its experience, questions and part of its research became one of the inputs to `M{Y}OGA JYOTISH`, but M{Y}OGA was substantially rebuilt on different epistemic, source-critical and mathematical foundations.

This book is preserved as a historical beta edition and an overview of an earlier research stage. It is **not** the current canon of `M{Y}OGA JYOTISH` or `MET[Ȧ]CADEMY OF HUMANITY`.

The edition preserves the source voice and hypotheses. Editorial intervention is deliberately light: typographic and grammatical cleanup, structural Markdown normalization, reduction of extraction/chat seams, and restoration of three evident extraction omissions (`Aquarius`, `10th house`, `Saturn`) directly from the historical `SAMYOGA_RAW.md` source corpus.
