Samyoga Jyotish · Book I

Historical beta edition · v1.0β · UA · 2026. Preserved as a record of an earlier research stage, not as current M{Y}OGA canon.

Migration notice

Samyoga Jyotish is a completed and closed research project. No further work is conducted under the Samyoga name. Its experience, questions and part of its research became one of the inputs to M{Y}OGA JYOTISH, but M{Y}OGA was substantially rebuilt on different epistemic, source-critical and mathematical foundations.

This edition should therefore be read as historical and overview material. It does not retroactively receive later M{Y}OGA rules or claims.

Edition

The source voice, hypotheses and humour were retained. Editing was intentionally light: grammar and typography, structural cleanup, reduction of extraction seams, and restoration of three evident omissions from the historical raw corpus: Aquarius, the 10th house and Saturn.

Verified package: 198-page A4 PDF using IBM Plex only, plus a Markdown edition with a clickable contents table. Full provenance, checksums and QA are recorded in the publication receipt.

Attachment boundary: the verified MD and PDF were prepared in this publication pass, but are not claimed as repository attachments until a safe binary/local-file transfer is verified. No synthetic download link is published.
