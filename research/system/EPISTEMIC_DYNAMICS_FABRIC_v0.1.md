This fabric is harvested from Science Aperture research notes SA004-01 through SA004-06 and the MET[Ȧ]CADEMY Manifest v1.2. It extends the research frontier without modifying sealed MSL 0.10.1 governance evidence.

## Purpose

The fabric gives the system six missing abilities: reconstruct events from evidence that arrives out of order; localize prediction failure before escalating to theory failure; mature anomalies through null and post-hoc audits; track diagnosticity of evidence; distinguish convergence from shared lineage; and learn from consequences in closed loops.

## P0 receipts

**EVENT_LINEAGE_RECEIPT** stores observed-now signal, historical traces, modalities, time offsets, candidate causal lineages, contradicting traces, missing evidence and next discriminator.

**PREDICTION_FAILURE_RECEIPT** decomposes failure through object class, local assumptions, boundary/environment, measurement pipeline, model and law. Escalation is local-first.

**ANOMALY_MATURATION_RECEIPT** separates observation status, interpretation status and model-pressure status; includes post-hoc/search-space metadata and null-model simulation.

**DIAGNOSTICITY_RECEIPT** records whether evidence merely exists or uniquely distinguishes one cause from alternatives.

**CONVERGENCE_RECEIPT** separates shared ancestry from independent convergence under shared constraints.

**CONSEQUENCE_RECEIPT** extends action receipts beyond execution success into world change, intended effect, side effect, feedback and policy/state update.

## Cross-cutting worldline

Every mutable research claim may have an **EPISTEMIC_WORLDLINE**:

`CLAIM_STATUS(t0) -> evidence/update -> CLAIM_STATUS(t1) -> ...`

Old statuses are not overwritten. The system must retain `why_once_plausible`, `update_reason`, `evidence_added`, `evidence_weakened` and `next_reopen_trigger`.

## Guards

`CO-OCCURRENCE + TIMING != CAUSALITY`

`PREDICTION FAILURE != THEORY FAILURE`

`ALTERNATIVE SURVIVED != ALTERNATIVE WON`

`NOMINAL SIGMA != EPISTEMIC WEIGHT`

`PATTERN AFTER TUNING != PREREGISTERED PREDICTION`

`THREE DEPENDENT WITNESSES != THREE INDEPENDENT WITNESSES`

`EVIDENCE EXISTS != EVIDENCE UNIQUELY IDENTIFIES CAUSE`

`SIMILAR FORM != SHARED LINEAGE`

`ADAPTIVE BEHAVIOUR != PROVEN SUBJECTIVE EXPERIENCE`

`OUTPUT QUALITY != CONSEQUENCE QUALITY`
