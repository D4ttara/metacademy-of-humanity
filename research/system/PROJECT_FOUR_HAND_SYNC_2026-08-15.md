# Project Four-Hand Sync · 2026-08-15

The project currently has four concurrent named workstreams. They are connected, but they are not merged into one authority surface.

## 1. MI6_TRANSLATION

MІЖ multilingual semantic-pressure laboratory.

Current observed upstream:
- Language Roadmap `v16`;
- System Integration Backlog `v5.1`;
- latest inspected pressure return: Modern Greek V3;
- supporting returns: Latin V5/L8 and Sanskrit V8.

Role: produce literary language bodies, semantic pressure observations and candidate MSL mechanisms.

Authority: `CANDIDATE_GENERATION_AND_TRANSLATION_RESEARCH`.

## 2. MYOGA_EXECUTABLE

M{Y}OGA / Astroprocessor executable evidence laboratory.

Lineage: `Varga → Relation → Time → Event → Prediction → Case Lab → Benchmark → Reference Corpus → Source Closure`.

Role: force source, provenance, precision, blind/holdout, negative-evidence and reproducibility rules into executable contracts.

Authority: `EXECUTABLE_RESEARCH_ONLY`; deterministic calculation does not certify interpretive truth.

## 3. SAMYOGA_HISTORICAL_PUBLICATION

SamYoga is a completed and closed predecessor research project. Book I v1.0β is preserved in the Historical Library. Its source identities remain stable for provenance.

Role: historical/publication lineage and research ancestry.

Authority: `HISTORICAL_PROVENANCE_ONLY`.

## 4. IMAGO_META_SYSTEM

Wider system architecture: IMAGO OS, MSL, Meta.Logic, MetaPass, MetaProcessor→MetaField evolution, WorkField and related system research.

Role: integrate validated contracts into the wider architecture through explicit transition gates.

Authority: no subsystem silently becomes system canon.

## Version-axis rule

`MSL 3.x` is the conceptual/pre-textbook axis. `MSL 0.9.xa1` is the executable research protocol/runtime axis. Mi6 Roadmap versions and M{Y}OGA engine versions are separate axes again.

`CONCEPTUAL VERSION != RUNTIME VERSION`.

## Convergence rule

Translation pressure may propose an MSL candidate. M{Y}OGA may demonstrate a reusable executable contract. SamYoga may preserve ancestry. The IMAGO meta-system may integrate a result only through explicit review/promotion.

`WORKSTREAM != CANON AUTHORITY`  
`PARALLEL WORK != SILENT MERGE`  
`CONVERGENCE REQUIRES RECEIPT`

## Contextual identity

Greek V3 contributed a useful general identity law: `CONTEXTUAL IDENTITY != GLOBAL EXCLUSIVITY`. A local name expansion may be correct inside one body without becoming the mandatory expansion in every other context.
