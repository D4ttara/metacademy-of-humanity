# Play / Game / Relation Field · 3×3 research matrix

Status: `OWNER_APPROVED_ACTIVE_RESEARCH`. The matrix compares **play** (open exploration), **game** (declared constraints) and **relation field** (participants plus changing conditions) across three lenses.

| Lens | Play | Game | Relation field |
|---|---|---|---|
| Time | exploratory tempo | turn / deadline | multiple clocks and negotiated pacing |
| Rule | provisional affordance | explicit rule | rules may be interpreted, contested or revised |
| Meaning | emergent gesture | scored outcome | meaning depends on participant history and observer frame |

Practical use: choose a game when auditability matters, play when candidate generation matters, and a relation field when the system must preserve context and disagreement. This is a design comparison, not a psychology or social physics claim.
