# Science / Metaphysics Mirror Protocol

Status: `OWNER_APPROVED_ACTIVE_RESEARCH`.

1. State the scientific observation and its method.  
2. State the metaphysical reading as a separately labelled candidate.  
3. Name the bridge: analogy, structural resonance, lived experience, tradition, or unknown.  
4. Name what would count as a limit or counterexample in each domain.  
5. Do not move evidence across the bridge without a new method.

The protocol neither demotes metaphysical inquiry to decoration nor presents it as a physical measurement. `STRUCTURAL_RESONANCE != PROOF`; `NOT_PROOF != NOT_WORTH_EXAMINING`.
