# Three SA003 Metaphysical Hypotheses

All three are `METAPHYSICAL_CLAIM / OPEN`, not scientific findings.

1. **Relational becoming:** what appears as time may be a local ordering relation among events rather than a universal container. Limit: no current physical derivation follows from this statement.
2. **Remainder-bearing world:** history may leave residues that participate in what can be recovered. Limit: this does not establish memory outside operational systems.
3. **Observer-participating horizon:** observation context can alter meaningful access to a process. Limit: epistemic participation does not by itself entail causal control of external matter.

The hypotheses are kept because they organize questions and counterquestions; they are not a licence to speak fog with a lab coat.
