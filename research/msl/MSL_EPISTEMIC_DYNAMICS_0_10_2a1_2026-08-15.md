# MSL EPISTEMIC DYNAMICS · 0.10.2a1

**Status:** EXPERIMENTAL CANDIDATE AFTER 0.10.1 GOVERNANCE ADJUDICATION

This version does not reopen or alter sealed 0.10.1 evidence. It creates a new prospective research layer for epistemic worldlines and consequence-aware receipts.

## Candidate operators

`event_lineage_join` -> build candidate causal histories from temporally and modally separated traces.

`failure_localize` -> escalate from object/input assumptions through environment/model to law only as evidence requires.

`anomaly_mature` -> audit search space, post-hoc tuning, dependency and null simulations before model-pressure claims.

`diagnosticity_score` -> separate support strength from specificity among competing causes.

`convergence_audit` -> distinguish answer similarity caused by shared lineage from convergence under shared constraints.

`consequence_close_loop` -> append observed world consequence to action lineage before policy/state update.

## Required ablations

Remove historical backsearch; remove null-model audit; remove diagnosticity; remove dependency metadata; remove closed-loop consequence. Each ablation must show whether the candidate mechanism contributes measurable value or merely grows another ministry.
