# MSL 0.9.9a1 · Method Governance

**Status:** EXECUTABLE RESEARCH FRONTIER · NOT STABLE CANON

MSL receives a domain-neutral governance layer from M{Y}OGA v0.14.

A failed or weak method is not silently repaired forever. A governance policy must be frozen before adjudicative evidence and must specify minimum batches, opportunities, cases, domain coverage and answer coverage.

New executable object: `MethodGovernanceReceipt`.

## Core laws

`POLICY PRECEDES ADJUDICATIVE EVIDENCE`

`RETIREMENT != ERASURE`

`RETIREMENT != PROOF OF FALSEHOOD`

`LOW COVERAGE != SUCCESS`

`PILOT != VALIDATION`

`REVIVAL REQUIRES NEW VERSION OR NEW EVIDENCE`

MSL keeps governance componentwise. A method cannot avoid retirement merely by abstaining on nearly everything, and a method cannot be retired from a source-feasibility pilot already seen by the researcher.

## Governance states

- `RETAIN_ACTIVE`
- `LOW_COVERAGE_REVIEW`
- `REVIEW_FOR_RETIREMENT`
- `INSUFFICIENT_GOVERNANCE_EVIDENCE`

These are governance states, not metaphysical truth classes.

`REVIEW_FOR_RETIREMENT != FALSE`

## Evidence eligibility

Governance must distinguish:
- exploratory / feasibility material;
- researcher-preexposed material;
- post-policy-freeze prospective evidence;
- evidence that satisfies declared coverage and corpus thresholds.

A policy written after seeing candidate adjudicative evidence cannot retroactively certify that evidence as governance-clean.

`POLICY TIME IS PART OF PROVENANCE`

## Current M{Y}OGA return

The v0.14 M{Y}OGA policy requires at least two post-freeze batches, twenty opportunities, three cases, five of six frozen domains, and a minimum method-coverage gate before direct null-comparison retirement review.

The D20/D30 feasibility pilot was researcher-preexposed before policy freeze and is therefore excluded from retirement adjudication. It may support source-pipeline feasibility only.

No method is retired at this frontier.
