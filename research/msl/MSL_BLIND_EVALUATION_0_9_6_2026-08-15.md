# MSL 0.9.6a1 · Blind Evaluation / Negative Result Protocol
## 2026-08-15

**Status:** EXECUTABLE RESEARCH FRONTIER · NOT STABLE CANON

The first preregistered M{Y}OGA round contributes domain-neutral validation contracts to MSL. The domain result itself does not become a universal MSL truth; the transferable value is the protocol for preserving what was and was not hidden, how imprecise source inputs were projected, how broad predictions are reported, and how negative results survive.

## 1. Blindness is typed

`MACHINE_BLIND != RESEARCHER_BLIND != CASE_SELECTION_BLIND`

A sealed payload proves only what was hidden from the machine path. It does not prove that the researcher did not see the information during discovery or selection.

Executable object: `BlindnessReceipt`.

## 2. Input projection is provenance

A day-level source is not silently converted into an exact instant.

`SOURCE PRECISION != PROJECTED INSTANT`

Every projection requires an `InputProjectionReceipt`. Sensitivity checks may compare alternate projections without pretending the original source was more precise.

## 3. Hit is not specificity

A multi-label prediction can include the revealed label while remaining broad.

`HIT != SPECIFICITY`

Specificity is reported separately via `SpecificityReceipt`. A post-hoc specificity diagnostic cannot rewrite a frozen verdict.

## 4. Negative result survives

`NOT_SUPPORTED` is a first-class result. It is not renamed to an “interesting tendency”, deleted, or averaged away.

`NEGATIVE RESULT STAYS`

Executable objects: `NegativeResultReceipt`, `BlindEvaluationReceipt`.

## 5. Null sensitivity remains visible

A deterministic null can depend on canonical input serialization or projection. That dependency is reported rather than hidden.

## 6. Upstream changes do not mutate a frozen experiment

During the v0.11 round, Mi6 translation upstream advanced to Integration Backlog v5.3, including new ΖΩΝΤΑΝΗ ΦΩΝΗ / Material Reality candidates. These are registered as new upstream candidates for a later Promotion Lab and are **not** retroactively injected into the frozen round.

`UPSTREAM DELTA != POSTHOC METHOD MUTATION`
