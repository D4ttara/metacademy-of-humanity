## Core distinction
`ADJUDICATION APERTURE OPEN != EFFICACY PROOF`

A method-governance state is a version-scoped operational decision, not an ontological verdict.

`RETAIN ACTIVE != TRUTH`
`REVIEW FOR RETIREMENT != FALSEHOOD`
`LOW COVERAGE REVIEW != METHOD FAILURE`
`GOVERNANCE STATUS IS VERSION-SCOPED`

## Cross-batch requirement
The adjudication receipt accepts only batches that reference the same frozen governance policy and occur after its freeze timestamp. It reports eligible batches, opportunities, distinct cases, represented domains and method decisions.

`CROSS-BATCH AGGREGATION REQUIRES FROZEN POLICY`

`LEGACY COMPARATOR != GOVERNANCE CANDIDATE`

## Source-sensitivity rejection
A candidate case may fail before prediction seal because its downstream output changes within declared source uncertainty. Such a rejection is provenance, not negative method evidence. The system must not shrink source uncertainty after observing instability merely to admit the case.

`SOURCE SENSITIVITY REJECTION != METHOD FAILURE`
`DO NOT NARROW SOURCE UNCERTAINTY AFTER SEEING INSTABILITY`
