IMAGO

Human-centred semantic computing research.

IMAGO investigates provenance, uncertainty, authority boundaries, continuity, non-action or abstention, and auditable receipts. It is presented here as an active technical programme, not as a completed superintelligence or a proof of AI consciousness.

Meta.Logic

Keep contradictions legible long enough to learn from them.

Meta.Logic works with contradictory models, hypotheses, unknowns, epistemic classes and model revision. The aim is not to make every claim equally strong; it is to keep source, scope, limit and uncertainty from being quietly erased.

Different ways of knowing can enter the same graph without pretending to be the same kind of evidence.

Sense moves through more than language.

M{Y}OGA JYOTISH · active research

Join without erasure. Measure without reduction.

M{Y}OGA JYOTISH is a research discipline where Jyotiṣa/Jyotish can meet Western astrological traditions, astronomical geometry, mathematics, philology, psychology, philosophy and metaphysics, lived experience and AI-assisted research without collapsing them into one evidence class.

{Y} is the JOIN FIELD: the shared Y belongs to both MY and YOGA. The discipline tracks what is preserved, transformed, added, lost, contradicted or newly revealed when systems meet.

MORPHO / M4M horizon

Adequate body for an invariant.

This research asks which representation or computational body is adequate for a declared task or invariant. Candidate bodies can be compared with simpler baselines and parked if they lose. Negative results are receipts, not basement furniture.

Public horizon only. No claim of a completed formal theory or public runtime is made here.

IKAR

Local experimental verification.

IKAR is a local experimental and verification harness: testable fixtures, receipts, comparisons and failed cases. It is not presented as an autonomous agent, public service or action authority.
