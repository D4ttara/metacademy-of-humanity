# Lost Field Reconstruction Protocol

Status: `ACTIVE_RESEARCH`. A lost field may be reconstructed only from declared remainders: surviving text, independent testimony, material trace, formal constraint, or reproducible effect. The output must be one of `RECOVERED_PARTIALLY`, `RECONSTRUCTED_CANDIDATE`, `UNRECOVERABLE`, or `UNKNOWN`.

Never convert coherence into recovery. A beautiful reconstruction without remainder is a new work, not a recovered source.
