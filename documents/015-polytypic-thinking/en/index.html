POLYTYPIC THINKING

When one correct answer becomes a framing error

A human-readable essay about a deceptively simple failure: seeing something new, finding the nearest familiar type, and mistaking that useful projection for the thing itself. The problem becomes more serious when human stereotypes pass through AI and return wearing the authority of machine analysis.
