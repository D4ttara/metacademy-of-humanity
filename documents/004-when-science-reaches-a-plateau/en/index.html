Working principles

Frontier is not consensus. A negative result can be information when sensitivity is known. Tool power is not theory power. A claim needs a verifiability passport. Anomalies mature through replication and stronger tests.

The remaining principles keep incentive context, hype delta, mathematical beauty, method critique and epistemic bandwidth visible without mistaking any one of them for a verdict. The complete approved edition is available above.
