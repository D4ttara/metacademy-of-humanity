How this atlas is read

Source language is preserved. Timestamps, advertising, greetings, navigation and other service noise are cleaned. Obvious automatic-subtitle errors may be corrected without intentionally rewriting the author’s thought. If a word cannot be recovered with confidence, the editor does not invent it.

MetaAcademy reflection is explicitly editorial and separate from the source. Digest items may carry sub-identifiers such as #NNN.A so an idea can be revisited without a spelunking expedition through a mountain of text.

Public mode: metadata, source links and concise editorial summaries. Full third-party transcript blocks are intentionally not republished here pending rights clearance.
