Why MetaAcademy?

Different ways of knowing do not need to become one thing to meet well.

Knowledge has more than one body: a scientific result, an engineering receipt, a lived experience, a tradition, a cultural image, a hypothesis, a contradiction, an open question. The work is not to flatten them into a soup, or let one worldview declare itself customs control for reality.

MetaAcademy is an institutional horizon for careful encounters: preserving terms, visible assumptions, provenance, limits, disagreement and what remains unknown.

Public alpha means the doorway is real while the building is still learning its own geometry. Stable claims and experiments remain visibly different.
