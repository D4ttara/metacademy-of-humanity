Why MET[Ȧ]CADEMY?

Different ways of knowing do not need to become one thing to meet well.

A scientific result, an engineering receipt, a lived experience, a tradition, a cultural image, a hypothesis, a contradiction and an open question have different evidence passports. The task is neither to flatten them into soup nor let one worldview become customs control for reality.

MET[Ȧ]CADEMY keeps provenance, limits, disagreement and the unknown visible.

Public alpha means the doorway is real while the building is still learning its geometry.
