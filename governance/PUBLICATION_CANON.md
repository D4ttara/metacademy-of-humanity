# MET[Ȧ]CADEMY publication canon

This is a publication, attribution, layout and storage canon. It does not define scientific, metaphysical or engineering truth.

## Attribution

Every new Academy publication carries this exact line:

`© 2026 Ievgen Karogod / Dattara · MET[Ȧ]CADEMY OF HUMANITY (MoH)`

An approved earlier edition is not cosmetically rebuilt merely to conform to a later layout rule.

## New numbered documents

New documents use the durable route `documents/<NNN>-<canonical-slug>/`. Each edition records language, version, source relation, date, SHA-256 and supersession relation in `publications/PUBLICATION_REGISTRY.yml`.

The visual series identity for a new document is: IBM Plex where available; a pale-blue left stripe; a small `(MoH)` plaque at left; a grey `MET[Ȧ]CADEMY OF HUMANITY` title one typographic step larger than the previous header; and a secondary, right-aligned sign strip exactly as written below.

`· (A) · {Ȧ} · [Ả] · {Ã} · (Ā) ·`

`[Ả]` has slightly stronger weight. The strip is secondary information, never the primary accent. Layout stays spacious, editorial and research-legible rather than ceremonial wallpaper.

## Equal public surface across the corpus

Publication date does not create a privileged class of Documents. A newer Document may lead the newspaper front page, but older public Documents remain first-class members of the corpus.

`NEWER != MORE CANONICAL`

`EQUAL PUBLICATION SURFACE != EQUAL EVIDENCE STATUS`

`DISCOVERY PARITY != GENRE COLLAPSE`

Every numbered public Document must have, where the underlying edition genuinely exists:

- a durable canonical reading route and explicit language identity;
- its real genre and status rather than a generic academic costume;
- a place in the complete public corpus and at least one meaningful research route or program when applicable;
- a route to the corpus atlas, research programs and guided Start Here surface;
- a tailored AI critique path that asks for counterexamples rather than applause;
- a public reader question / disagreement route;
- citation support appropriate to its genre; Scholar metadata is used only where the publication qualifies for that surface;
- machine-readable discovery through the relevant sitemap, `llms.txt`, structured data and public corpus/program passports;
- provenance and publication-boundary discipline inherited from the registry and Publication Firewall;
- a visible relationship to neighbouring corpus material without implying that sequence equals proof or supersession.

A Field Manifesto is not silently relabelled a technical report. An exploratory research candidate is not described as deployed infrastructure. A cultural artifact does not become scientific evidence because it shares navigation with a research essay.

The public interface may be consistent. The epistemic passport must remain specific.

## Corpus admission and future Documents

A new numbered Document is not publication-complete merely because its PDF or HTML exists. Before public promotion it should be able to answer five questions: what is this, where does it sit, what is its provenance, how can it be challenged, and what machine-readable trace identifies it?

The Pages and pull-request validation pipelines should fail when a new corpus-wide layer claims full coverage but silently omits an existing public Document. The preferred repair is to expand the corpus map, not to weaken the verifier.

`DECLARED SUCCESS != VERIFIED RESULT`

## Storage and links

Small public artifacts are stored with their document in the repository. A very large external source may remain external only when the registry names its URL, version, SHA-256 and provenance. Third-party text is linked or summarized within rights boundaries, not silently republished.
