# MET[Ȧ]CADEMY publication canon

This is a publication, attribution, layout and storage canon. It does not define scientific, metaphysical or engineering truth.

## Attribution

Every new Academy publication carries this exact line:

`© 2026 Ievgen Karogod / Dattara · MET[Ȧ]CADEMY OF HUMANITY (MoH)`

An approved earlier edition is not cosmetically rebuilt merely to conform to a later layout rule.

## Document identity

Global integer documents retain durable routes `documents/<NNN>-<canonical-slug>/`. They are historical publication identities, not a promise that all future research bodies share one chronology. Provenance-based research branches may use lineage IDs such as `SA004-01`. Exact policy: `governance/DOCUMENT_IDENTITY_AND_NUMBERING.md`.

## Visual family

New numbered Academy print/PDF documents use IBM Plex where available; a pale-blue left stripe; a small `(MoH)` plaque at left; a grey Academy wordmark; and a secondary right-aligned sign strip:

Working production rules live in `governance/brand/METACADEMY_ACADEMY_DOCUMENT_PRINT_STANDARD_v0.1_UA.md`; measured implementation tokens are not automatically eternal brand constants.

## Registry and lineage

Each edition records language, version, source relation, date, SHA-256 and supersession relation in `publications/PUBLICATION_REGISTRY.yml`. Source-derived series also record their parent source ID. A source digest is provenance, not certification authority for the scientific claim.

## Storage and links

Small public artifacts are stored with their document in the repository. A very large external source may remain external only when the registry names its URL, version, SHA-256 and provenance. Third-party text is linked or summarized within rights boundaries, not silently republished.
