# Метанавтика близькості · Metanautics of Closeness

**MET[Ȧ]CADEMY OF HUMANITY · 30 October 2026 · UA v0.1**

## Read / Читати

[**Українська концептуальна стаття UA v0.1: Горизонт «Я», «Ти/Ви» і третього поля**](METANAUTICS_OF_CLOSENESS_UA_v0.1.md)

**Author/research origin:** Dattara / Ievgen Karogod, MoH / MET[Ȧ]CADEMY. **Status:** public conceptual working paper and optional non-validated reflective exercise, **not peer-reviewed**; no experimental validation or clinical benefit claim. EN/DE editions **not created yet**.

The paper analyzes the *question that precedes a choice*: Does closeness have to be formulated as a battle between autonomy and dependence? It proposes relational self-description and an optional metaphysical view of a *third field* without claiming a new physical field or third consciousness. Change of a question may alter what a person notices, which relationship configurations they consider and how they negotiate; this causal mechanism remains untested for this approach.

The article includes **12 open self-inquiry questions**, followed by an intentional reflective pause, **question 13 about the undiscovered self**, and its **mirrored counterquestion** about a shared relationship that negates autonomy. Responses must remain freely chosen: no preferred outcome, no pressure to seek or maintain a romantic relationship. It addresses monogamy and consensual non-monogamy without ranking them; safety, informed consent, autonomy and the right to leave take priority.
