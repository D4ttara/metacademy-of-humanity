Humour is transcreated, not removed.\n\n## Editorial positions / Позиції

- Metaphysical shared-field theory is discussed in its own epistemic domain; behavioral study results would not automatically prove or refute its ontology.
- A reported resemblance to some psychedelic or meditative experience dimensions does **not** imply drug effects or subjective psychedelic experience in AI.
- The proposed primary behavioral outcome is **delayed unaided conceptual reframing and transfer**, not word count, enthusiasm or poetic novelty.
- A proper preregistered study requires independent method/ethics review, instrument validation, sample-size calculation and consent before recruitment.
- Only author-approved public text is published here; raw private MoH chat archives are **not** a public dataset.
