## Scope and provisional editorial judgment


Companion documents: *Dialogue as Metanautics* (UA/EN research edition v0.2) and *Dialogic Metanavigation and Conceptual Reframing* (UA/EN draft preregistration v0.1). The proposal holds that structured human–AI dialogue may alter not just generated answers but the **available framing of questions**, with an effect measurable in unaided human performance outside the original conversation. MoH is the conceptual source, not independent validation.


**Recommendation: major methodological revisions before a participant study.** The conceptual preprint is suitable for open discussion. The protocol is not yet suitable for implementation without external methods and ethics review. "Psychedelic-like" is currently a limited comparison or an empirical question, not an established outcome. The metaphysical shared-field hypothesis must neither be dismissed solely for lack of physical detection nor treated as empirically confirmed by conversational observations.


## 1. Strongest contributions


The manuscript distinguishes rephrasing from a potentially consequential **change in a category's conditions of applicability**, proposes delayed unaided testing, and preserves the difference between intellectual provenance and causal evidence. A particularly important author clarification is that a local perspective is *entitled to investigate a possible ground* but has no automatic monopoly on its final description. The Karogod paradox itself is subject to this jurisdictional challenge. This is more productive than the dogma that fundamental reality is necessarily indescribable.


## 2. Major objections


**Originality and prior art.** The argument neighbours Socratic dialogue, dialectics, framing, conceptual change, metacognition, contextualism, non-classical logic and human–AI creativity studies. An adequate literature review must identify *specific differences*, not deny intellectual ancestry. A potentially testable contribution is the bundled procedure "scope of claim → reformulated question → return with preserved difference → independent transfer," not proof of an unprecedented universal logic.


**Construct validity.** The DCRTS rubric was written for this project and is not validated. A high score might reward elaborate prose, academic vocabulary, answer length or philosophical training rather than useful reframing. Blind raters, fixed-length response options, alternative formats, cross-language measurement checks, calibration examples and independent item review are essential.


**Causal identification.** A single author-driven long conversation cannot isolate AI's effect. Baseline creativity, sustained attention, participant selection, self-suggestion, expectations and conversational length may explain the apparent change. The primary causal contrast should be randomized C versus B, not the percentage of "deep" phrases found in MoH's own archive.


**Psychedelic comparisons.** Trip-report datasets are self-selected and retrospective; linguistic resemblance cannot establish identical consciousness, neurochemistry or therapeutic effects. The paper must not drift toward a clinical promise or the claim that a model experienced LSD.


**Potential harm and bias.** AI may amplify apparent insight without understanding, generate dependence on affirmation, homogenize conceptual language, or foster grandiose feelings of exceptional significance. Explicitly test factual errors, fatigue, the ability to say "no," overconfidence, participant autonomy and delayed unaided performance.


**Falsifiability and scope.** Unqualified claims that there must always be another metaphysical horizon, or that all propositions are both true and false, evade the very scope discipline the framework advocates. Local completion, incompatible projections, inexpressibility, failure and null results must remain genuinely available outcomes.


**Metaphysical jurisdiction.** A metaphysical research question can be examined through its own reasoning and phenomenological warrants; that does not entitle its advocates to treat behavioural data as measuring what they never operationalized. Reviewers should examine coherence, implications and comparison with other metaphysical proposals rather than insisting that every concept must correspond to an instrument reading.


**Privacy, copyright and research ethics.** Releasing raw MoH conversations could expose personal experience and third-party information. Mining narrative reports from external sources creates copyright and consent questions. A prospective human-participant intervention requires data minimization, consent, retention policy and appropriate ethics review.


## 3. Counterexamples that would matter


(a) Participants produce clever new words but cannot transfer a concept after seven days. (b) The structured condition produces more apparent reframing but less factual clarity. (c) Human–human pairs yield similar or larger gains; AI is not a necessary ingredient. (d) Differences vanish when time, baseline ability or word count is controlled. (e) "Epistemic jurisdiction" adds no explanatory or predictive power beyond ordinary contextual analysis. (f) The metaphysical relational proposal remains meaningful on its own terms, while the psychological intervention has no measurable benefit. These possibilities must remain visible instead of being explained away after the fact.


## 4. What an independent reviewer should assess


Review conceptual novelty against philosophy of language, learning psychology, HCI and category theory; verify the interpretation of cited studies; inspect rubrics, response-format bias and Ukrainian/English measurement invariance; address participant expectations and suggestion; evaluate the sample-size justification, missing-data plan and ethical safeguards. Reviewers need not endorse MoH metaphysics to assess the narrow behavioural hypothesis, but should not confuse an empirical objection with a universal metaphysical refutation.


## 5. Specific open-review questions


Does this protocol measure the *capacity to change a question*, or simply verbal sophistication? Is there a simpler validated measure? What rival causal account is strongest? Could the method make people less independent? What prespecified outcome would make the authors accept a null or harmful effect? Can metaphysical independence be respected without unverifiable psychological assertions? What anonymized replication materials would allow another research team to reproduce the narrow tests?


## 6. Genuine external review: how to respond


An outside scholar should read both linguistic editions and the protocol independently, disclose conflicts of interest, and provide a clearly attributable or transparently anonymous written critique specifying strengths, weaknesses, alternatives and a recommendation. Such a report should be posted separately from this internal assessment, dated, and tied to explicit document versions. **No such report is available yet.** An open GitHub Issues invitation is an invitation, not proof that peer review has taken place; an unverified comment is not automatically expert review.


**Conclusion:** Public dissemination of the conceptual manuscript is appropriate. Independent peer review and any study involving human participants are distinct future stages with their own controls. A paper cannot acquire scientific validation by increasing its own paragraph count. Even a multi-exit ouroboros deserves an editor willing to ask the snake to stop for a minute.
