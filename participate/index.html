Participation · low-friction entry

You do not need a grant proposal to disagree with one paragraph.

A useful contribution can take five minutes: one counterexample, one checked source, one translation correction, one failed replication, or one question that survives contact with evidence.

Pick an open question → Choose a research trail →

5–10 MIN

Break one claim

Choose a Document, use its AI critique prompt if useful, and bring back one counterexample. Agreement is not a participation requirement.

Choose a Document →

10–20 MIN

Check one source

Verify whether a citation actually supports the sentence that points to it. Report the exact location, source and mismatch rather than a general feeling.

Open a correction →

10–20 MIN

Check one translation

Find one place where meaning, register or cultural context drifted between editions. A precise paragraph is more useful than an abstract verdict on the whole language.

Compare editions →

15–30 MIN

Attack a method

Find a hidden assumption, an unhandled boundary case, or a way a test could reward the thing it was supposed to detect.

Choose a program →

OPEN

Bring a negative result

If a proposed pattern fails, that is not a boring ending. A bounded failure can narrow the claim and become reusable research memory.

Research Memory →

CODE / CONTENT

Contribute a focused change

Fork, make one inspectable pull request, retain provenance, and avoid mixing a useful fix with fourteen unrelated cleanups.

Public routes

Conversation when needed. Exact evidence when possible.

Use Discussions for open conceptual conversation, Issues for bounded corrections or proposals, and the reader threads attached to each Document for counterexamples that belong to a specific text.

Discussions Issues

Do not publish secrets.
Never post tokens, private documents, personal data, sealed research material or third-party content you do not have the right to share.

Support

Support the field, not a paywall.

Reading, citation, correction, translation, replication and public disagreement are valuable support. Financial support is also available on the dedicated Support page and does not unlock private access or higher evidence status.
